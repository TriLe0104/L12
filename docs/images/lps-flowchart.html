Adjust

gap = (allocated − consumed) / allocated. Stay in [min, max]. Leftover envelope brings up more racks.

  • Reduce Idle > 20% slack. Drop toward usage, ≥ min.
  • Hold 5–20% slack. No write.
  • Increase Hot < 5% slack. Feed, ≤ max.
  • Enable Leftover ≥ min. Power another rack.
